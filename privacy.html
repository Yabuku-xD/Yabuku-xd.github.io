Privacy

Last updated 24 September 2026

What this site is

A personal site. There are no accounts, no payments, and no forms that save what you type.

I don’t run ads, analytics, or tracking cookies. I don’t sell anyone’s information or build profiles of visitors.

What gets collected

Cloudflare hosts the site and may log your IP address, browser, the page you asked for, and the time. Those logs keep the site running and block abuse.

The listening page shows my Spotify history. A Cloudflare Worker fetches it with my own token. Visiting doesn’t touch your Spotify account.

If you email me, I have your message. I keep it until I’ve replied. Links to other sites follow their own rules.

Requests

For privacy questions, or to have me delete an email you sent, write to shyamalankannan@gmail.com with “Privacy” in the subject. Other ways to reach me are on the contact page.
