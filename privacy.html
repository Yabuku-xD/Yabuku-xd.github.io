what this site is

shyamalankannan.com is a personal portfolio. it describes my work, open-source contributions, and how to email me. it is not a product, store, or account system. you cannot create a login, pay for anything, or submit a form that stores your data on this origin.

i do not run advertising networks, first-party analytics dashboards, or marketing pixels on these pages. i do not sell personal information. i do not build visitor profiles.

what is collected

the hosting and cdn provider (cloudflare) may receive standard request logs: ip address, user agent, requested path, and timestamp. those logs exist to operate the site and respond to abuse. they are not used to target ads.

the listening page fetches my own spotify listening stats through a cloudflare worker that uses my refresh token. that api returns my public-facing listening data, not yours. visiting the page does not connect your spotify account.

if you email me, i receive whatever you put in the message, including your email address. i keep correspondence only as long as needed to reply. outbound links go to github, linkedin, x, spotify, and other third-party sites that have their own policies.

requests

to ask a privacy question, or to request that i delete an email you sent, write to shyamalankannan@gmail.com with the subject “privacy”. more contact options are on contact.
