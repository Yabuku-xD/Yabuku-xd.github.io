privacy

last updated: 21 august 2026

what this site is

this is a personal site. no accounts, no payments, no forms that store your data.

i don't run ads or analytics. i don't sell personal information or build visitor profiles.

what is collected

cloudflare may log ip address, user agent, path, and time. those logs run the site and stop abuse.

the listening page fetches my spotify stats through a worker that uses my refresh token. that is my listening data, not yours. visiting does not connect your account.

if you email me, i receive the message. i keep it until i've replied. links off this site follow those sites' rules.
