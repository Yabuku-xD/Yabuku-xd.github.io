# Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

A personal site. No accounts, no payments, and no forms that save what you type.

Shyamalan Kannan doesn't run ads, analytics, or tracking cookies. He doesn't sell anyone's information or build visitor profiles.

## What gets collected

Cloudflare hosts the site and may log your IP address, browser, the page you asked for, and the time. Those logs keep the site running and block abuse.

The listening page shows his Spotify history. A Cloudflare Worker fetches it with his own token. Visiting doesn't touch your Spotify account.

If you email him, he has your message. He keeps it until he has replied. Links to other sites follow their own rules.

## Requests

For privacy questions, or to have an email you sent deleted, write to [shyamalankannan@gmail.com](mailto:shyamalankannan@gmail.com?subject=Privacy) with "Privacy" in the subject. Other ways to reach him: [contact](https://shyamalankannan.com/contact.md).
